## 1. Academic & Scientific Purpose

DopplerGPS is developed and published by **Dyne Research** strictly as an open-source scientific instrument. Its sole intended purpose is to advance academic research in atmospheric physics, acoustic propagation, signal processing education, and legitimate, defensive open-source intelligence (OSINT) methodology.

The techniques implemented in this software utilize:
- Public, unencrypted civil aviation transponder broadcasts (ADS-B Out, 1090 MHz),
- Public terrain elevation datasets (e.g., USGS 3DEP, SRTM), and
- Classical acoustic Doppler equations first formulated by Christian Doppler (1842).

## 4. Non-Infallibility & Safety-Critical Exclusion

Acoustic Doppler geolocation is highly sensitive to real-world variables, including wind velocity profiles, atmospheric thermal inversions, terrain reflections (multipath), and transponder timestamp discrepancies.

- **Non-Certifiable Output:** Output coordinates represent mathematical optimization approximations and **do not constitute definitive, certifiable, or forensic ground truth**.
- **No Safety-Critical Use:** DopplerGPS must **never** be used for life-critical navigation, emergency search and rescue, air traffic separation, or any application where error or failure could result in death, personal injury, or environmental harm.
